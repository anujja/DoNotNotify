Take control of your Android notifications with DoNotNotify — the simple, privacy-first way to block unwanted alerts and focus on what matters.

Tired of constant pings, spam, and clutter? DoNotNotify lets you create powerful yet easy rules to block notifications from specific apps or by matching text — even using regular expressions. No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Everything runs locally on your device.

Key features:

🛑 Block by app or by text: Silence a whole app, or only the notifications that contain certain words or match a regular expression. Or allow only the ones you care about and block the rest.

📚 Stack: Gather a chatty app's notifications into one tidy group you can expand, instead of a constant stream.

⏳ Throttle: Let the first notification through, then block repeats for as long as you choose. Perfect for apps that ping you every few seconds.

🕒 Right time, right place: Limit any rule to certain hours, or to when you're (or aren't) using your phone.

✨ Easy rule wizard: Tap a notification in your history to create a rule for it. As you type, DoNotNotify shows which of that app's notifications your rule will match.

📦 Pre-built rules: Ready-made rules for popular apps are added automatically, and more are shared by the community.

📜 View notification history: See all notifications your device has received, even the ones that were dismissed or blocked — so you never miss anything important.

🔒 100% private: All processing happens on your phone — no internet required.

⚡ Lightweight & battery-friendly: Runs silently in the background without draining power.

Privacy by design:

DoNotNotify never collects or shares your data. There are no analytics, ads, or cloud servers — your notification data never leaves your device. Notification history is kept only on your phone and is never included in backups; Android may back up your rules and settings if system backup is turned on.

DoNotNotify is open source (MIT License): https://github.com/anujja/DoNotNotify

Whether you want fewer distractions, better focus, or simply a cleaner notification shade, DoNotNotify gives you peace and control — effortlessly.
